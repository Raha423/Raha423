Built for dependable recovery

Everything you need to know about Backup Center, its security model and how your data moves.

Apira VPS Backup Center

Backups you can verify, protect and recover with confidence.

Backup Center creates real compressed archives, validates every result with SHA-256 and keeps recovery under your control—from automated scheduling to protected restore staging.

Version 1.0.0
Maintained by Apira VPS
Archive format TAR.GZ + SHA-256

Verified archives

Every archive receives a SHA-256 checksum, clear status tracking and retention handling you can audit.

Secure by default

Authentication, CSRF protection, encrypted credentials and controlled downloads protect sensitive operations.

Flexible delivery

Send backups through Telegram, strict-host-key SFTP, protected email or encrypted S3-compatible storage.

How Backup Center protects your data

A clear path from source to recovery

01 Collect

Package selected server paths into a compressed archive.

02 Verify

Generate a checksum and record the result for auditing.

03 Deliver

Send only to destinations you explicitly configure.

04 Recover

Download securely or extract into protected staging.

Your server remains the source of truth. Credentials and archive data stay on your server unless you deliberately configure an external delivery destination.
